# Business Questions

Dataset: REES46 eCommerce behavior data, October 2019 (Nov 2019 to be added).

## Funnel and conversion
1. What is the overall view to cart to purchase conversion rate?
2. How does conversion differ by category and by brand?
3. Where is the biggest drop off in the funnel, and how does it vary by hour and weekday?

## Revenue and products
4. Which categories and brands generate the most revenue?
5. What are the top 20 products by revenue, and how concentrated is revenue (Pareto)?
6. What is the average order value, and how does it vary by category?

## Customers
7. Who are the high value customers? (RFM segmentation)
8. What share of revenue comes from repeat versus one time buyers?
9. What is the cart abandonment rate, and which categories abandon most?

## Retention and time
10. How does user retention look by first activity cohort? (more meaningful once Nov is added)

## Tools per question
SQL: 1 to 6, 8, 9 | Python: 7, 10 | Power BI: dashboard views of 1, 4, 7, 9
